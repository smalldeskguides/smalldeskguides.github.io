Small desk organization

Simple systems for small desks

Short, practical guides for keeping a small desk usable, starting with what you already own.

  • Give a small desk three homes

    Split the desk into a work zone, a daily-reach zone and a reset spot.

  • The five-minute desk reset

    A timed end-of-day routine that leaves the desk ready for tomorrow.

  • Label both ends of every desk cable

    Trace, tag both ends and leave slack, using tape you already have.

  • Before you buy a monitor stand, check three things

    Screen height, distance and keyboard height, checked before you buy.

  • Three trays for the paper pile

    Act, File, Recycle: decide once per paper, clear weekly.

  • Use the wall above your desk

    Three bands on the wall: see, reach, rarely use.
