Small desk, more space

Use the wall above your desk

A small desk runs out of surface fast, but the wall above it is usually empty. Split it into three bands by how often you use things.

The three bands

1. Eye level: things you need to see

Put a calendar, today’s notes or a short list where your eyes land when you look up from the screen. Seeing them is the point, so don’t put anything here you never read.

2. Within reach: things you grab daily

Hang headphones, scissors or a charging cable on hooks just above the desk, where you can reach without standing up. Things that hang free your desk surface.

3. Up high: things you rarely use

Spare supplies and boxes of records can go on a high shelf. They’re out of the way, and you can still find them.

Set it up

  1. Sit in your chair and reach up without leaning. Mark the highest comfortable point with a small piece of tape. Your within-reach band goes below it.
  2. Look straight ahead and mark eye level. Things to see go around that mark.
  3. Above both marks is the up-high band.
  4. Move one item at a time from the desk to its band. If nothing on the wall fits an item, it may not need to be at the desk at all; see give a small desk three homes.

Renting or hanging heavy things? Check the weight rating on any removable hook or strip and follow its surface instructions. Anything heavy that hangs above where you sit needs proper wall anchors, not adhesive.
