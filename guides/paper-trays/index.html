Paper pile

Three trays for the paper pile

Paper piles grow because every sheet is waiting for a decision. Three trays let you make that decision once, the first time you pick a paper up.

The three trays

Act

This tray is for anything that needs something from you: bills to pay, forms to sign, letters to answer. Keep it small and in your daily-reach zone so it stays visible.

File

This tray is for things to keep that don’t need action: warranties, tax documents, records. It’s a waiting spot until they go into a folder or box.

Recycle

This tray is for anything you no longer need, like flyers, envelopes and inserts. Shred anything with personal or financial information first, as the FTC advises.

Set it up

  1. Use what you have. Box lids, shallow boxes or folders all work.
  2. Label each one with tape and a marker: Act, File, Recycle.
  3. Put Act within reach, File near wherever your folders live, and Recycle next to the bin.
  4. Sort the current pile once, one sheet at a time, choosing a tray for each.

Keep it working

  • Once a week, clear the Act tray, move File papers into folders and empty Recycle. This fits the weekly version of the five-minute desk reset.
  • No “maybe” tray. If you can’t decide, it goes in Act, since deciding is the action.
  • Watch the Act tray. If it overflows, it’s become a second pile, so schedule time to clear it.

Source: FTC, 5 ways to help protect your identity.
